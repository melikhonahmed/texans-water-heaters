Redesign the current website into a modern, premium, high-converting plumbing and water heater service website.

Important: Use all of the existing website content, information, services, headings, contact details, offers, testimonials, FAQs, and calls-to-action from the current website. Do not remove important content or replace the existing business information with placeholder content. The goal is to improve the design, layout, user experience, and conversion rate while preserving the current content.

Design Direction

Create a website that feels:

Modern and professional
Premium and trustworthy
Clean and easy to navigate
Local-service focused
High-converting
Mobile-first and fully responsive
Fast and accessible

Avoid an outdated or overly corporate appearance. Avoid generic AI-generated layouts and excessive rounded cards. The design should feel like a professionally designed website for an established local plumbing and water heater company.

Visual Style

Use the existing brand identity and colors as inspiration, especially:

Dark navy blue
Bright blue
Red accents for urgent actions and promotions
White and light gray backgrounds

Improve the visual hierarchy and use more whitespace.

Use strong, modern typography with clear differences between headings and body text.

The design should use:

Clean grid layouts
Large, confident headings
Professional service imagery
Subtle shadows
Smooth border radius where appropriate
Modern icons
Clear visual hierarchy
Subtle animations and hover effects

Do not make every section look like a separate card.
